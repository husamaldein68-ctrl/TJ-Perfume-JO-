Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about concentrations, ordering, and delivery.

What is the oil concentration?

All TJ Perfumes are crafted at Eau de Parfum and Extrait concentrations (25% to 35% pure fragrance oil). This guarantees minimum 8 to 14 hours of performance depending on skin chemistry and climate.

How does delivery work in Jordan?

We deliver within 24 hours inside Amman, and 24 to 48 hours for Zarqa, Irbid, Salt, and all other southern and northern governorates with cash on delivery.

What are your official bottle sizes and prices?

Our pricing is transparent and standardized:

• 100ml Flacon: 20 JOD
